AI Usage Policy

Last Updated: July 1, 2026

Welcome to embti. To provide our users with deep, interactive, and highly customized personality analytics and social engagement experiences, our Application integrates advanced Artificial Intelligence technologies, specifically Google Gemini AI.

This AI Usage Policy explains how Gemini AI functions within the Application, how your data interacts with AI models, and the limitations of AI-generated content.

Core AI Integration: We utilize Google's Gemini large language models via secure API endpoints to power interactive personality dialogues, generate dynamic compatibility breakdowns, and translate raw quiz metrics into engaging self-exploration narratives.

1. How We Use Gemini AI

Gemini AI is incorporated into several key pillars of the platform:

  • **Dynamic Profile Generation:** Summarizing and expanding upon your MBTI quiz answers to offer nuanced, textual breakdowns of your personality traits.
  • **AI-Powered Chat & Companions:** Powering virtual personality dialogues, enabling you to test communication styles or simulate conversations based on different MBTI profiles.
  • **Social Matching Insights:** Generating textual summaries explaining the unique interpersonal dynamics, potential strengths, and communication friction points between you and matched profiles.

2. Data Privacy and AI Data Flow

Your privacy is central to our implementation. When interacting with our AI features, data flows under strict guidelines:

  • **Anonymized Inputs Only:** We only send contextually relevant, non-identifying data (such as quiz score distributions, selected interest tags, or your active chat inputs) to the Google Gemini API.
  • **No Personal Identifiers Shared:** We explicitly do not share your real name, email address, password, precise location, or financial details with the Google Gemini API.
  • **Strict Confidentiality:** Data transmitted to the API is processed in accordance with Google's enterprise confidentiality standards and is not used to train public foundational models or sold to third-party brokers.

3. Important Limitations of Generative AI

While Gemini AI is exceptionally powerful, generative technology carries inherent limitations that you must understand:

  • **Potential for Hallucinations:** AI can occasionally generate text that is inaccurate, fictitious, or speculative. Treat AI-generated insights as a creative mirror rather than scientific or medical absolute facts.
  • **Subjectivity and Nuance:** Personality theories are inherently subjective. AI interpretations are meant to stimulate self-reflection and entertainment, not to serve as rigid diagnostic guidelines.
  • **No Professional Advice:** AI-generated outputs do not under any circumstances constitute professional counseling, psychiatric evaluation, career therapy, or clinical advice.

4. User Guidelines and Prohibited AI Inputs

When interacting with the AI features or inputting prompts into our conversational tools, you strictly agree not to:

  • Attempt to inject malicious scripts, manipulate model instructions ("jailbreak"), or force the AI to bypass established safety filters.
  • Input text containing explicit, abusive, harassing, defamatory, or hateful content.
  • Provide sensitive personal data or the private information of third parties within public AI prompt blocks.
